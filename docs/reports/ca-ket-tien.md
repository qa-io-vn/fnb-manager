# Ca & két tiền — shifts and cash drawer

Route `#/chu-de/ca-ket-tien?range=<preset>` or `?from=…&to=…`, plus `&store=<id>,<id>` · title "Ca & két tiền" · default the last 28 days, at most 92 · built in phase 10b (#155; the cashier card #192) on the range rules of [Doanh thu](doanh-thu.md#range) (ADR-035); the staff app's cash-count card added in phase 14b (#225, `631aec0`).

Ca & két tiền puts each shift beside the store's other shifts: what it sold, how its drawer counted against the cash expected, and whether its voids, removed lines or edits after the close stand out against the store's own recent shifts. Since 14b a fifth card puts the cash the staff app counted at the close beside the POS drawer ([The staff app's cash counts](#the-staff-apps-cash-counts-phase-14b)). It is a prompt to look at a shift, never a verdict on a person.

## Range

The 92-day maximum is the limit of `shift_adjustments` (and of the 56 trailing days its scores read). A longer preset (52 tuần, or Năm nay from April) or a link with longer dates is shortened to its last 92 days with the page's notice "Chủ đề này xem tối đa 92 ngày nên chỉ hiện 92 ngày cuối."; the address then holds the two dates. ‹ and › step by the range's own length. A tab from another theme carries the range moved to the last 92 days (`moveRangeToLimit`), without the notice. The functions are always called with at most the last 92 days of the range, even if a context longer than the definition allows were built.

## Calls

| Key | Call |
|---|---|
| `coverage` | `store_coverage(S, R.from, R.to)` |
| `shifts` | `shift_review(S, R.from, R.to)`, thresholds left out so the tenant's cash thresholds apply (D3) |
| `adjustments` | `shift_adjustments(S, R.from, R.to)`, the z threshold left out so the tenant's applies (2 by default) |
| `settings` | `report_settings_of(S)` |
| `memberShare` | `member_share_by_shift(S, R.from, R.to)` (phase 16a, #236) |
| `memberSharePrevious` | `member_share_by_shift(S, …)` over the range of equal length just before `R` |

There are no follow-up calls. Each card waits only for the calls it reads (`shiftsNeeds`): every card for `coverage`, the sales card for `shifts`, the drawer card for `shifts` and `settings`, the adjustments card for `adjustments` and `settings`, the cashier card for `shifts` and `adjustments`. A slow or failed `shift_adjustments` call therefore leaves the sales and drawer cards standing, and a failed `shift_review` call the adjustments card; either holds back the cashier card. A failed `coverage` call holds back all five cards and a failed `settings` call the drawer and adjustments cards. The member share card waits for `memberShare` and `memberSharePrevious` only, so it neither holds back nor waits for the other four.

The flags are the functions' own: the drawer card counts exactly the rows `shift_review` flags under the tenant's thresholds, the same shifts `cash_variance_flags` flags (pgTAP of phase 10a; the live comparison is #166), and the adjustments card counts exactly the rows `shift_adjustments` flags (a rate's z-score above the threshold, or an edit after the close). The reasons read as one message per combination (`theme.shifts.reasons.*`: hủy, xóa món, sửa sau khi đóng ca).

CSV files are named `ca-ket-tien-<card>-<from>_<to>.csv` (the cashier card's `ca-ket-tien-cashiers-<from>_<to>.csv`) and the drawer card's table per cashier `ca-ket-tien-drawer-<from>_<to>-2.csv`, UTF-8 with a BOM, in the device's comma or semicolon format.

The cashier card (#192) groups the shifts of both calls, joined by shift id, so every shift counts once even if only one function returned it: its net revenue is the sum of `shift_review`'s `sales_amount`, its flagged shifts and Σ\|variance\| are those of the drawer card's table per cashier (`shiftTotals`), and its voids, removed lines and edits after close are the sums of `shift_adjustments`' rows; each column of the table therefore sums to the total of the range's shifts. Its flags are the drawer flags (`flagged_shifts`), as in Tháng's "most flags"; the adjustment flags stay with the adjustments card, and the table shows the counts behind them. The column "Ca lệch két vượt ngưỡng" is the same figure, with the same label, as in the drawer card's table per cashier. A cashier is the masked value (`shownCashier`, `cashierCell`, `cashierShownValue`), so every e-mail login and every empty cashier fall into one "chưa rõ thu ngân" row; that row stays in the table but never leads, ties or counts as a cashier. The card first decides, from the distinct trimmed and lower-cased raw cashier values, only whether they differ (nothing of them is shown), and shows no ranking in two cases, with "—" as headline and an empty table:

- **Shared login.** With at least 2 shifts and at most one distinct value in each store, as on a POS where all cashiers share one login: "Trong {range}, cả {n} ca đều ghi cùng một tài khoản thu ngân …" (or, when stores differ, "mỗi cửa hàng ghi mọi ca dưới một tài khoản thu ngân duy nhất …") and the notice that figures per cashier need each cashier's own POS login (#11).
- **No display name.** Otherwise, when every value shows as "chưa rõ thu ngân" (e-mail logins or empty values): "… thu ngân của cả {n} ca đều không có tên hiển thị (để trống hoặc là địa chỉ e-mail) …" and the notice to give each cashier login a display name in the POS. No pseudonym is made up.

## The member share per shift (phase 16a)

The card of the phone-capture week (#237, FR-LOY-01): how often each shift attaches a customer to the bill with «Thêm khách hàng» before it prints ([sop-them-khach-hang.md](../loyalty/sop-them-khach-hang.md)). It reads `member_share_by_shift` ([schema.md](../schema.md#thematic-report-functions-phase-10)) in live mode and its port in `@fnb/demo-analytics` in the demo, whose generator attaches a synthetic `0900000xxx` phone to a seeded share of invoices from 1 October 2026 on.

- **Shifts.** The function numbers the shifts by opening time within their store and business date, exactly as `app.shift_number` and `shiftNumbers` do, and the card labels them with `shiftNumberValue` of `packages/reports/src/model/shifts.ts`; it never receives a shift id, the POS shift name, a cashier or a phone. A shift without invoices is listed with zeros. An invoice sold after midnight stays with the shift it names, on that shift's date; an invoice that names no shift of its store goes into the day's "Không rõ ca" row.
- **Totals.** The headline, the chip and the slots sum the rows' counts before dividing, so a busy shift weighs more than a quiet one, as the metric registry asks for every multi-store figure.
- **Not a ranking of people.** The sentence names a shift number, the slot to remind; there is no per-cashier view (individual logins are the owner's action #11).
- **Link.** The notices of the capture card of Chất lượng dữ liệu ("Định danh khách và đồng ý") carry "Xem tỷ lệ hóa đơn có hội viên theo ca" (`report.action.theme_member_share`), which opens this page.
- **Before the capture week.** On the real data no invoice carries a phone yet (3,538 synced invoices on 2026-11-19), so the card shows 0 % and the plain sentence; after the week (#11) the owner reads it per store against the epic's target of at least 20 invoices with a phone per store.
- **CSV.** `ca-ket-tien-member_share-<from>_<to>.csv`, the table's columns, no phone.

## The staff app's cash counts (phase 14b)

"Số đếm két trên ứng dụng" (`CashCountThemeCard` in `apps/web/src/operations/theme-cards.tsx`, test id `operations-card-cash-counts`) is not a card of the theme definition: the page passes it to `ThemePage` as an extra card (`extraCards`, `src/pages/themes/ca-ket-tien/ca-ket-tien-page.tsx`), so it comes after the four cards above, reads the operations source instead of the report source, and the theme's calls, needs and markers do not apply to it.

- **Call.** `cash_count_review(S, from, to)` through `cashCounts` of the operations source (`src/operations/supabase-operations-source.ts`), over the last 92 days of the range (`lastDaysWindow` with `SHIFT_THEME_RANGE_DAYS`); on this page the range is at most 92 days, so the window is the range itself. The function returns every synced shift of the stores and days the member may read, as "Ca n" by opening order and never with the POS shift name or the cashier, with its active count from the staff app, plus the counts not matched to a shift yet; the difference counted minus expected and the flags follow the drawer card's rule under the tenant's thresholds (the function is in [api.md](../api.md); a count is matched to the shift whose close is nearest within 60 minutes, as `shifts_without_count` explains).
- **Headline.** The shifts whose count is over a threshold (display series `cash_count_flags`, related `shifts_without_count`), captioned "{counted}/{closed} ca đã đóng có số đếm"; "—" when the range has no shift.
- **Sentence.** "{counted} trong {closed} ca đã đóng có số đếm trên ứng dụng; {over} ca lệch quá ngưỡng và {missing} ca chưa đếm.", or "Chưa có ca nào trong khoảng này." No chips.
- **Notice and link.** The rule in words: the count's difference is the count less the expected cash (the opening float plus the cash taken), judged by the same thresholds as this page, and "Két POS" is what the cashier typed on the POS at the close. Its link "Xem lịch sử két tiền" opens Vận hành → Két tiền (`#/van-hanh/ket-tien`) with the same dates and stores.
- **Table.** One row per shift and per unmatched count, the first 10 shown: (store, with several stores), date, shift, closed at (the store's wall clock), "Đếm trên ứng dụng", "Két POS", "Tiền mặt dự kiến", "Chênh lệch đếm", the state ("Chưa đếm", "Chưa khớp ca", "Đã ghi nhận", "POS không có số dự kiến", "Vượt ngưỡng", "Trong ngưỡng"), the thresholds it went over, the person who counted (their roster name, "Bạn", or their role; never a POS cashier name), when, and how many times the count was reopened. CSV `ca-ket-tien-dem-ket-<from>_<to>.csv`.
- **Who sees it.** Owners and managers (`staffPageAccess`); staff, viewers, the publisher and a membership that could not be read get the card's title with "Mục này chỉ dành cho chủ và quản lý." and no figure. In the demo, which has no staff app, it says "Mục này cần ứng dụng nhân viên; bản demo không có nên chưa có số liệu." and shows no figure or form. A failed call shows "Không tải được thẻ này." with "Thử lại"; when the function refuses the window for its size (`22023`) the card says "Quá nhiều dòng để xem một lần; chọn ít cửa hàng hơn hoặc khoảng ngắn hơn." instead.

## Shift labels and cashiers

- A shift reads "Ca 1", "Ca 2", … by its opening time within its store and business day, ties broken by its external id and a shift without an opening time last: `shiftNumbers` of `packages/reports/src/model/shifts.ts` (#48), the digest's rule. Each card numbers the shifts of its own call; both functions return the shifts of `agg_shift_cash` in the range, so a shift reads the same in every card (unit and engine smoke tests).
- The POS shift name and external id are never shown, because on the real data the name holds the cashier's POS login e-mail. With several stores the labels in sentences and charts carry the store ("Ca 1 ngày 20/10/2026 ở …").
- A cashier value that is empty or shaped like an e-mail address shows as "chưa rõ thu ngân" (`shownCashier`, `cashierCell`, `cashierShownValue`); in the drawer card's table per cashier and in the cashier card such shifts fall into that one group, which the cashier card never names as the leader.
- The same rules hold on screen, in print and in every CSV, because print and CSV take their text from the same table models; the drawer card's sentence, table and chart are built by the helpers Thanh toán's drawer card uses (`shiftLabeller`, `shiftOnLabel`, `drawerTable`, `drawerChart`, `drawerFlaggedPart`, `drawerThresholdsPart` in `themes/payments/cards/drawer.ts`), not copies of them.

## States and markers

- **No shift.** Without shifts in the range each card says "Chưa có ca nào trong {range}.", shows "—" and a notice to check that cashiers open and close shifts on the POS.
- **Shared login or no display name.** The cashier card shows "—", no ranking and one of its two notices, as described under Cards.
- **No z-score.** A z-score needs 3 earlier shifts of the store in the 56 days before whose rates differ. Without them (a young store, a store's first weeks of data, or earlier shifts that all have the same rate, such as no removed line at all) the score is empty, the table shows "—" and the adjustments card says why in a notice whenever any shift of the range lacks either score.
- **No edit or void synced.** Voids, removed lines and edits come from the POS adjustment events; a store whose POS sends none shows rates of 0 and no z-score.
- **Catch-up pending.** Every card carries "đang cập nhật" while a catch-up range overlaps the page's range, since the shift aggregates are written by the refresh.
- **Payouts.** A cash payout is not synced from the POS, so it reads as a shortage (the `cash_variance` note under "Giải thích chỉ số").
