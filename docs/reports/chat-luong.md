# Chất lượng dữ liệu — data quality

Route `#/chu-de/chat-luong?range=<preset>` or `?from=…&to=…`, plus `&store=<id>,<id>` · title "Chất lượng dữ liệu" · default the last 28 days, at most 400 · built in phase 10b (#158) on the range rules of [Doanh thu](doanh-thu.md#range) (ADR-035).

Chất lượng dữ liệu lists what is missing or wrong in the data and says, for every finding, where to fix it: in the POS ("Sửa trong POS: …", `theme.quality.fix.pos.*`), in Settings (a hint naming the page and tab, `theme.quality.fix.settings.*`, and the card's Settings link, `report.action.settings_*`), or by running the sync again (`theme.quality.fix.syncDay`, `syncDays`, `syncMaster`). The page changes nothing itself. It shows counts and shares only: no customer, phone number or name of a person appears on it, in its CSV files or in print (NFR-PRV-06).

## Calls

| Key | Call |
|---|---|
| `coverage` | `store_coverage(S, R.from, R.to)` |
| `days` | `period_series(S, g, R.from, R.to, true)` with `g = seriesGrain(R.days, \|S\|, true, 'day')`: the checked and matched days per store, by day while days × (stores + 1) ≤ 900 (up to 300 days for 2 stores, 225 for 3), then by week, then by month, so the call never needs more than 900 rows |
| `items` | `data_quality_items(S, R.from, R.to)` |
| `summary` | `data_quality_summary(S, R.from, R.to)` |
| `payments` | `data_quality_payments(S, R.from, R.to)` |
| `margins` | `item_margins(S, R.from, R.to)`, for `cost_coverage` exactly as Thực đơn reads it |

All six calls go through the report source, so the page reads the SQL functions when signed in and the demo engine's ports in the demo. The live check holds `data_quality_items` to 150 ms, `data_quality_summary` to 300 ms and `data_quality_payments` to 50 ms at the default range (`packages/reports/test/live/theme-live-check.test.ts`).

## Cards

The compliance, uncategorised and verification cards are wide, so their "Cách sửa" column shows in full on a computer; a column of text longer than 24 characters wraps within its width in a wide card, so its last column stays reachable on a phone.

**Uncategorised, the same on both pages.** An item counts as uncategorised when its effective type is not a known item type, it is not a catalogue modifier and it has no override: `isUncategorisedItem` of `@fnb/reports`, which Phân loại's Chưa phân loại list (`useUncategorisedSales`) uses too. Overrides are read when the functions run, so after a re-mapping on Phân loại the item is gone from this card the next time the page loads, an in-app navigation included, because the save invalidates the tenant's analytics (acceptance A6).

**Days not checked.** By day, a day is not checked when it has invoices and no check, as `store_coverage` counts it, so the table and the headline agree. Past the daily limit a bucket's days not matched are its checked days less its matched days, and its days not checked are its days with sales less its checked days (only in a bucket with invoices), so a check on a day without sales can hide a day not checked inside the same bucket; the headline comes from `store_coverage` and stays exact.

**Store findings (FR-CMP-10).** A store has a finding when its e-invoice status is anything but `configured`; when its receipt numbering is not recorded (`unknown`) or disagrees with the data (recorded `daily` while the numbers do not restart daily, or `yearly` or `never` while they do; the data's reading is `receipt_restarts_daily` of `data_quality_summary`, null under 20 numbered days in the last 120, and then nothing is compared); when the member feature is recorded as off; and when its opening hours, seats or opening date are missing. The status words are those of Cài đặt → Cửa hàng ("Chưa rõ" for a status not yet recorded). `data_quality_summary` also returns `unattributed_events`, the invoice events no shift owns; the build spec gives it no place on this page, so it is not shown.

CSV files are named `chat-luong-<card>-<from>_<to>.csv`, the second table `-2`, UTF-8 with a BOM, in the device's dialect. The finding, state and hint columns are written in words, as on screen.

## States and markers

- **Nothing to fix.** Each card says so in a sentence ("Mọi món bán trong … đều đã có nhóm.", "Mọi cửa hàng đã ghi đủ …") with a count of 0 and no table or link.
- **No data.** Without sales, payments or invoices in the range the card says so and shows "—" (NFR-UX-06).
- **No customer of the list.** The customer card names that state instead of a 0 % coverage (FR-THM-09).
- **Catch-up pending.** The cards that read item or payment aggregates (`uncategorised`, `costs`, `payments`) carry "đang cập nhật" while a catch-up range overlaps the page's range or a store's aggregates lag; the others never do.
- **Verified badge.** Every card carries the coverage marker: "đã đối soát", "chưa đối soát" or, in the demo, "dữ liệu mẫu".

## The demo

`MOCK-S2` is recorded `not_configured` for e-invoices, with its receipt numbering unknown and no opening hours or seats, so the compliance card flags it (acceptance A7); `MOCK-S1` has every field. The demo's receipt numbers start with "HD", so the numbering in the data reads "Chưa đủ dữ liệu" for both stores and nothing is compared. "Hạt bí rang" is the uncategorised item, no item has a cost, two store-days are unverified over the last 28 days, no payment method counts as Khác, no catalogue modifier is sold as an item and no invoice links to a customer of the list (the demo has phone numbers only).
