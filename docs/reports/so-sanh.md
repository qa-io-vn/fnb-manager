# So sánh cửa hàng — store comparison

Route `#/chu-de/so-sanh?range=<preset>` or `?from=…&to=…`, plus `&store=<id>,<id>` · title "So sánh cửa hàng" · default the last 28 days, at most 400 · built in phase 10b (#193) on the range rules of [Doanh thu](doanh-thu.md#range) (ADR-035).

So sánh puts the selected stores side by side: the same figures for each store over the range, the shape of each store's days against its own average, and each store's first weeks aligned on its own opening, so that a gap between stores can be told apart from a difference in age or in calendar. It needs at least two stores; with one, every card says "Chọn ít nhất hai cửa hàng để so sánh." and no call but coverage runs.

## Calls

`S` is the selected stores, `R` the range, `R.prev` the range of equal length just before it.

| Key | Call |
|---|---|
| `coverage` | `store_coverage(S, R.from, R.to)`, always |
| `totals` | `range_totals(S, [R.from, R.prev.from], [R.to, R.prev.to])`, from two stores: a block for all stores, then one per store |
| `productivity` | `opening_productivity(S, R.from, R.to)`, from two stores |
| `days` | `period_series(S, g, R.from, R.to, true)`, from two stores, with `g = seriesGrain(R.days, stores, true, R.days ≤ 92 ? 'day' : 'week')`: by day up to 92 days while buckets × (stores + 1) stays within 900 rows (a 92-day range turns weekly from 9 stores), otherwise by week, or by month when even weeks would pass 900 |
| follow-up `totalsLfl.previous` | `range_totals(covered, [R.from, R.prev.from], [R.to, R.prev.to])` over the stores whose history starts on or before `R.prev.from`, only while some but not all selected stores do (Doanh thu's like-for-like rule) |
| follow-up `since.<storeId>` | `period_series([store], 'day', history_start, min(history_start + 7K − 1, asOf), false)` for each selected store with at least one complete week since its history start; `K` = the smaller of 26 and the complete weeks of the youngest such store |

No new SQL and no new port: `range_totals` and `opening_productivity` are the 10a functions (and their demo ports, #91 and #92), `store_coverage` and `period_series` phase 9's. The history start is coverage's `history_start`, the later of the opening date and the first day with sales ([metrics.md](../metrics.md#coverage-and-like-for-like)).

## Cards

| Card | Headline | Sentence | Table and chart |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chỉ số theo cửa hàng (`kpis`) | the leading store's share of the selected stores' net revenue, captioned "{store} dẫn đầu doanh thu thuần" (display series `store_net_share`; the card explains the eleven metrics below) | the widest gap in average ticket, then in items per ticket, each as the higher store's value against the lower one's; "gần như bằng nhau" when the gap would read 0,0 % or the stores are equal; while the all-stores change is like for like, the note "Dòng Tất cả cửa hàng so với kỳ trước chỉ tính cùng cửa hàng (k/N)…" | store, net revenue, its change against the range before, invoices, average ticket, its change, items per ticket, average item price, cash share, discount rate, invoices with a voucher, customer capture rate, revenue per opening hour, RevPASH: one row per store by net revenue, then "Tất cả cửa hàng" from the all-stores block, whose ratios come from the summed totals (never an average of the stores' ratios) · bars of net revenue per store, the range before beside this one. Full width |
| Cùng ngày, so sánh dáng (`same_day`) | the gap between the highest and the lowest store index on the day (or week, or month) the stores were furthest apart, captioned "chênh lệch chỉ số giữa các cửa hàng, lớn nhất ngày …" (display series `store_day_gap`, with `store_day_index` beside it) | "Các cửa hàng lệch nhau nhiều nhất ngày …: {store} bán bằng … lần mức trung bình của mình, {store} bằng … lần." | day (or week, or month), store, net revenue, index; newest first, opening on 14 rows · a line per store of its index, with a dashed line at 1 |
| Từ ngày khai trương (`since_opening`) | the youngest store's average net revenue per day in week K since its opening, captioned "tuần thứ K của {store}"; the subtitle says how many weeks are aligned | "Tuần thứ k sau khai trương: {store} đạt …% so với {other} cùng tuần." at the latest week k where neither store's week holds a Tết day, `{other}` being the oldest other store with such a week; when that is not week K, "Các tuần sau tuần thứ k có ngày Tết ở một trong hai cửa hàng nên không dùng để so sánh."; the store alone when no other store has a comparable week | week k, store, from, to, days with sales, net revenue, average per day, and "có ngày Tết" for a week holding a Tết day (the column appears only then); latest week first, opening on 14 rows · a line per store over weeks 1 to K (`since_opening_net`) |

The index of a store on a day is its net revenue that day ÷ its own average net revenue per day with sales over `R`; for a week or a month bucket, the bucket's net revenue per day with sales is used, so a week cut by the range's edge or a closed day does not read as a weak one. A day without sales has no index. Week k since opening covers `history_start + 7(k − 1)` to `history_start + 7k − 1` and its average per day is its net ÷ its days with sales, Năm's since-opening block rule ([nam.md](nam.md)); a week holds a Tết day when one of its day rows has `holiday_kind = 'tet'` or `tet_days > 0`.

Lines take the five categorical chart colours (`chart-1` to `chart-5`) in store order; from the sixth store on the colours repeat on dashed lines, so up to ten stores never share both colour and line style; the legend and the table name every store. The previous range's bars stay `chart-muted`.

CSV files are named `so-sanh-<card>-<from>_<to>.csv`, UTF-8 with a BOM.

## States and markers

- **One store.** Every card says "Chọn ít nhất hai cửa hàng để so sánh." with "—" as its headline, no table and no chart, and only the coverage call runs.
- **Opening hours or seats missing.** Revenue per opening hour shows "chưa thiết lập" (`report.unavailable.not_configured.previous`, the shared "not set up" words) in its cell for a store with sales and no opening hours, and RevPASH for a store without opening hours or seats; the all-stores row follows `opening_productivity`, which is null while any store with sales lacks them. The card links to Cài đặt → Cửa hàng ("Nhập giờ mở cửa và số ghế"). A store without sales in the range shows "—", not "chưa thiết lập". In the demo the young store has neither. A state in a figure's cell sorts after every number, in both directions.
- **Young store.** A store whose history starts after the first day of the range before reads "chưa có dữ liệu kỳ trước" in both change columns. While only some stores are covered, the all-stores row is labelled "Tất cả cửa hàng (so với kỳ trước: cùng cửa hàng)", its changes compare the covered stores over both ranges (`totalsLfl.previous`), and the sentence ends with "… chỉ tính cùng cửa hàng (k/N) …"; when none is covered it reads "chưa có dữ liệu kỳ trước" ([metrics.md](../metrics.md#coverage-and-like-for-like)).
- **Less than one complete week.** A store whose history started fewer than seven days before the as-of day has "—" in every week of the since-opening table and no line; the note "{store} có số liệu từ {date}, chưa đủ một tuần trọn vẹn nên để trống." names it, or "{stores} chưa đủ một tuần trọn vẹn…" names them all when there are several. K is taken from the youngest store that has a complete week. Without any such store the card says "Chưa cửa hàng nào đủ một tuần trọn vẹn kể từ khai trương."
- **Tết.** The since-opening comparison skips any week holding a Tết day at either store and says so; the table marks those weeks. The same-day index keeps Tết days, so the day the stores went most apart can be a Tết day, which the date shows.
- **No common day.** When no two stores sold on the same day (or week), the same-day card says so instead of naming one.
- **No data.** Without sales in the range the figures and same-day cards say "Chưa có doanh thu nào trong …" and show "—"; the since-opening card does not follow the range and keeps its weeks.
- **Catch-up pending.** The figures card reads the cash share from payment aggregates, so it carries "đang cập nhật" while a catch-up range overlaps the page's range.
