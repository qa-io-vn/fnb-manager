# Khuyến mãi & giảm giá — promotions and discounts

Route `#/chu-de/khuyen-mai?range=<preset>` or `?from=…&to=…`, plus `&store=<id>,<id>`, `&promo=<kind>:<key>` and `&cmp=prior_weeks|sister_store` when they differ from their defaults · title "Khuyến mãi & giảm giá" · default the last 28 days, at most 400 · built in phase 10b (#191) on the range rules of [Doanh thu](doanh-thu.md#range) (ADR-035); the member rewards card added in phase 18b (#274, `0ca91d3`).

Khuyến mãi shows what discounts and vouchers cost against gross revenue, how often invoices use a voucher, and whether one promotion brought revenue beyond the general trend: its gross lift, its discount cost and the net of the two, always together. After these three cards come the promotion catalogue with the lucky-draw warning and the two rule cards, the maximum discount and the discount days in the year (#153). The rule cards inform; they do not say that a rule was broken (ADR-021). The seventh card, "Ưu đãi thành viên đã đổi", counts the loyalty rewards members redeemed and what they cost (#274).

Range totals are written by the refresh, so the first three cards carry "đang cập nhật" while `store_coverage.stale_days > 0` or a catch-up range overlaps the page's range. The catalogue, rule and member rewards cards read the facts through their own functions and never carry it.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Values | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `promo` | `<kind>:<key>` of a promotion of `promotionsW92` (a select inside the lift card, largest discount first, each named with its kind, "Giảm giá hóa đơn không tên" for an unnamed one) | the promotion with the largest discount in `W92` |
| `cmp` | `baseline` ("Cùng thứ các tuần trước"), `prior_weeks` ("Các tuần trước chương trình"), `sister_store` ("Cửa hàng không chạy chương trình"), a radio group inside the lift card | `baseline` |

`sister_store` is disabled, with "Mọi cửa hàng đang chọn đều chạy chương trình này nên không có cửa hàng để so sánh." under the group and tied to the option, once the lift has answered with no control store (`control_store_count = 0`): every selected store ran the promotion in the window or in the pre-period weeks before it, which is always the case for a single store. A link that asks for it then drops `cmp` with a replace, as for any value the answered call does not offer. Changing either parameter pushes one history entry and asks only for the lift again. While only `cmp` changes, the options keep the state of the last answered lift (the control-store count does not depend on the comparator), so the option does not flicker; another promotion, range or store selection shows every option until its lift answers.

The lift card's controls sit between its headline and its chips and are hidden in print; the subtitle names the promotion, so a printed card still says what was measured.

CSV files are named `khuyen-mai-<card>-<from>_<to>.csv` and the components table `khuyen-mai-discount_cost-<from>_<to>-2.csv`, UTF-8 with a BOM.

**What the member rewards card counts.** `redemption_summary` (`supabase/migrations/20261119320000_redemption_reconciliation.sql`) groups the redemptions held on the business days of the window, by the store's business date of the hold, per visible store and reward. "Lượt đổi" counts them all; "Đã dùng" those a synced invoice confirmed, "Đang giữ" those still waiting and "Đã hoàn lại" those released with their stamps or points given back. Only the used ones spend stamps and points and cost anything: the reward cost is the payment discount of the confirming invoice on the fallback rail (`pos_payment_discount`) and its voucher amount on the CRM code rails, so a free item rung as a 0-price line costs 0 here. "Hóa đơn có thành viên" are the store's invoices of the window that earned for a member and were not clawed back, counted once per store however many rewards the store has. The function runs as the caller, so staff and the publisher get no row; it takes at most 50 stores and 92 days and answers at most 900 rows. The card reads only these counts and amounts: no member, phone or code appears on it.

## Rules

Every date, cap and keyword comes from `promotionRules('VN')` in `@fnb/core` (#101, `packages/core/src/rules/promotion-rules.ts`), read through the helpers of `packages/reports/src/themes/rules.ts` and `themes/promotions/rules.ts`; none is written in the cards, and the page always passes the cap to `promotion_compliance` from the constants (the SQL declares `cap_rate numeric default 0.5` only as a fallback for other callers, and holds no date). The theme has no country of its own yet, so it reads the Vietnamese rules (`PROMOTION_RULES_COUNTRY`). Each legal sentence names the instrument and article (`theme.promotions.rules.*` builds "Thông tư 39/2025/TT-BCT, Điều 4" or "Nghị định 81/2018/NĐ-CP, Điều 13 và 19"), states the date the sources were checked (12/11/2026) and says it is not legal advice.

| Rule | In force | Basis | What the cards do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discount cap 50 % (`discount_cap`) | 22/05/2018 – 30/06/2025, then from 01/07/2025 | Decree 81/2018/NĐ-CP Art. 7, then Circular 39/2025/TT-BCT Art. 4; a legal-watch item, because Decree 239/2026/NĐ-CP is reported to make the Ministry of Industry and Trade responsible for the caps and no newer circular was found on the check date | a discount counts as over the cap only strictly between the cap and the whole amount before it, and only item and bill discounts are counted; gift lines and fully free invoices are counted apart, and vouchers, which the rules treat as another form of promotion with a limit of their own, are listed apart as information (to be confirmed by #50). State-run concentrated programmes allow 100 % (`concentratedMaxDiscountRate`, added to both versions by #153); their dates are not data, so the card's note says every day is checked against the cap |
| 120 discount days per item and year (`discount_days`) | 22/05/2018 – 25/06/2026 | Decree 81/2018/NĐ-CP Art. 10; reported repealed from 26/06/2026 by Decree 239/2026/NĐ-CP, not yet confirmed (#49) | only price-discount days count: a line below 100 % or a bill discount below 100 % on an invoice holding the item; gift and voucher days are shown beside them and never added. A range ending after 25/06/2026 shows the count as information only with the reported-repeal sentence; items are called out only for the days up to 25/06/2026 (whole years before 2026) |
| Lucky-draw registration (`lucky_draw_registration`) | from 22/05/2018 | Decree 81/2018/NĐ-CP Art. 13 and 19 | the names of promotions used in `R` or active in the catalogue are matched against the keywords (bốc thăm, quay số, quay thưởng, rút thăm, trúng thưởng, may mắn, vòng quay, cào trúng, bốc lì xì, rút lì xì, lucky draw, prize draw, raffle) as whole words in any case and Unicode form, and a name without tone marks is a possible match. The warning names the first match ("và {others} chương trình khác" for the rest) and says such a programme must be registered with the provincial Department of Industry and Trade, or with the Ministry when it runs in several provinces, that Decree 239/2026/NĐ-CP is reported to have changed this procedure (how winners are witnessed and the registration dossier, as the rule's note records), with the basis and the check date |

When #49 verifies the official text and #50 amends ADR-021, only the dated constants and the sentences that name Decree 239/2026/NĐ-CP (`theme.promotions.ruleDays.repealed`, `theme.promotions.catalogue.luckyDraw`) or word the cap (`theme.promotions.ruleCap.subtitle`, `.vouchers`) change.

## States and markers

- **No promotion in `W92`.** No lift is asked for; the card says "Không có khuyến mãi nào trong {days} ngày cuối kỳ…", shows "—" and a notice to choose a range in which a promotion ran.
- **No test store, no control store, too few control days.** `no_test_store`, `no_control_store` (with `sister_store`) and `few_control_days` (fewer than 7 days without the promotion, with `baseline` or `prior_weeks`) show "—" without a caption, a warning notice with the flag's sentence, which says what to try, and "Chưa đo được hiệu quả của {promotion}…" followed by the next flag. The advice follows what can work: `few_control_days` suggests a store that did not run the promotion only while one is selected (`lift.flagNoStore.few_control_days` otherwise, which suggests a range with days without it), and `no_control_store` suggests the weeks before only when the window has at least 7 days without the promotion (`lift.flagDaily.no_control_store` otherwise). A plain before-and-after figure is never shown: without a counterfactual there is no lift, net or caption.
- **Other flags.** `few_promo_days`, `few_reference_days`, `unmeasured_days`, `control_differs_in_size`, `excluded_days_in_window` and `other_promotions` each have a sentence; the first one present closes the sentence. A flag the page does not know is left out rather than shown as a key.
- **Young store.** Its last-year chips read "chưa có dữ liệu năm trước"; with one young store among several, the chips compare the covered stores ("cùng cửa hàng (k/N)").
- **No cap rule for the range.** No compliance call; the cap card shows "—" and "Không có mức giảm tối đa nào áp dụng cho …". With the Vietnamese rules this happens only before 22/05/2018, earlier than any data.
- **No discount.** The cap card shows 0 with "Không có giảm giá nào trong … để đối chiếu." and no table; the days card shows 0, "năm {year}" and "Không món nào được giảm giá bán trong …".
- **A year after the 120-day limit.** From 2027 no second call is made and the days card has no over-the-limit sentence.
- **A year before 2023.** `item_discount_days` accepts years from 2023, so no discount-day call is made; the days card states the rule and its basis without a count sentence.
- **Demo rules.** The demo dresses one catalogue promotion that no sale points to, `PROMO-05`, as "Vòng quay may mắn" (`DEMO_LUCKY_DRAW_PROMOTION` in `packages/demo-analytics/src/dressing.ts`, applied in `demoPromotionCatalogue`, which also seeds the parity harness, so both engines read the same name and no number changes), so the catalogue card shows the lucky-draw warning on every range. Over the end-to-end clock's 28 days no item or bill discount goes over the cap (headline 0), "Voucher 20k" takes more than 50 % off the rest of a few small invoices and is listed apart with its own sentence and table, the whole-invoice row is last, no item passes 120 price-discount days before 26/06/2026, and four catalogue promotions have not been used for more than 90 days. A range ending on 25/06/2026 shows the limit in force.
- **No member reward.** With no redemption in the window the member rewards card shows 0 ₫ with its chip, "Chưa có lượt đổi ưu đãi nào trong {range}." and no table or chart; with one store the table has no store column.
- **Member rewards over a long range.** A range, or a range before, longer than 92 days reads the last 92 days of the range against the 92 days just before them, with the subtitle "{days} ngày cuối kỳ" and the chip "so với {days} ngày trước".
- **Member rewards in the demo.** The demo has no loyalty programme, so the card asks for nothing (`redemptionCalls` plans no call for a sample context), shows "—" without a chip, table or chart and says "Chưa có dữ liệu ưu đãi thành viên: bản demo không có chương trình khách hàng thân thiết." The demo engine still answers `redemption_summary` with no row (`packages/demo-analytics/src/functions/redemption-summary.ts`), with the function's 92-day limit.
- **Demo.** The demo's promotions run almost every day, so for the presets the lift shows the `few_control_days` state and `sister_store` is disabled. One window is measurable: for both stores, `from=2026-03-03&to=2026-06-02`, the bill discount "Giảm 5% hóa đơn" against `prior_weeks` shows a net loss with its gross lift, cost and trend, and `sister_store` is offered there because `MOCK-S2` had not opened. Every card carries "dữ liệu mẫu".
