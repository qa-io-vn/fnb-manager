# Staff Mini App (`@fnb/zalo-staff`)

Purpose and audience: the staff Zalo Mini App of track B, one build per brand from configuration, hidden from Zalo's search and opened by link or QR code (`mini_apps.browsable` false). For developers of the staff screens and for reviewers of Zalo's design rules. Phase 13 (#208) built the scaffold: the four tabs and the sign-in states, tested in a browser with a mocked Zalo SDK and a stand-in session. Phase 14 adds the entry (#215: onboarding, the staff notice, pairing, "từ chối", Privacy and Terms) and the home (#217); nothing has run inside Zalo yet, because the owner's Zalo accounts do not exist yet (#8).

Last updated 2026-11-19 (phase 15: Thông báo, requests and the approval queue, Nhận thông báo)

## What it shows

- Until the person is signed in, the entry (#215, `src/onboarding/`) replaces the whole app, without the bottom tabs:
  - **Onboarding**, on a first open and before any Zalo identity call: an internal staff tool, why a Zalo login is needed, what is kept (the Zalo id and display name; the phone only if linked later), one primary action "Tiếp tục", and "Quyền riêng tư" and "Điều khoản sử dụng" as popups. A person with a stored session, even an expired one, skips it.
  - **The staff notice** from `zalo-session`'s unpaired answer: "Đồng ý" leads to pairing (the acceptance is recorded by the pairing request itself), "Từ chối" to the refusal screen. With no published notice, a plain screen says to tell the manager.
  - **Pairing**: a 6-digit numeric field (numeric keypad, one-time-code autofill); "Ghép nối" shows its loading state at once; a wrong, used or expired code gets one message ("Mã không đúng hoặc đã hết hạn…"); after too many attempts, a countdown of the wait with the button disabled; a notice published meanwhile is shown again and the code is kept; success shows "Ghép nối thành công" for 1.5 s and opens Trang chủ.
  - **"Từ chối"**: stays usable, shows the tenant's contact instructions as plain text when the session answer carries them (otherwise "hãy hỏi trực tiếp quản lý cửa hàng"), and "Xem lại thông báo" leads back.
  - **A newer notice for a paired person**: `zalo-session`'s notice_required answer, or, with a stored fresh session, `my_staff_notice` at every open; the notice comes before anything else and "Đồng ý" calls `accept_staff_notice`. A session the API refuses (401 or 403, for example after a deactivation) is dropped and the app starts again at the notice instead of showing an error.
- Four bottom tabs once signed in: Trang chủ, Ca làm, Thông báo and Cá nhân ("Home", "Shifts", "Notices", "Me" in English). The first opens first; Thông báo lists the announcements (#228, below).
- Ca làm (#221, #224, `src/shift/`) for the member's store (a picker when there are several), with the shift phase from the store's opening hours (Mở ca until two hours after opening, Đóng ca from two hours before closing, Giữa ca between) and one primary action: the due checklist, else "Đóng ca".
  - **Danh sách việc** (`src/checklists/`): `due_checklists` of the store, the current phase first ("Đến giờ"), each with its items, photo items and state (not started, yours, someone else's, done at HH:mm). A run starts with `start_checklist_run` (one shared request even on a double tap; a refusal says whether someone else has it open, it is retired or not allowed) and shows the template version it read, so a checklist changed in the dashboard appears at the next run. Items: a checkbox, a number with its unit (decimal keypad), and for a photo item a one-line purpose, then Zalo's camera (`chooseImage`); refusing the camera leaves the item open with a hint and the rest usable. The photo is resized on the device to a JPEG of at most 1,280 px and 1 MB with its orientation baked in (`resizePhoto`), uploaded with visible progress into the run's `photo_bucket` and `photo_prefix`, then saved with `save_checklist_answer`; a failed upload keeps the photo for "Thử lại". Saves of one item are queued, so a tap that comes while the number is saving is kept. "Hoàn thành" asks first when items are left, calls `finish_checklist_run` and shows the result for 1.5 s; a finished run, or someone else's, is read-only. A finished checklist can start another round ("Làm lượt mới").
  - **Đóng ca** (`src/shift-close/`): the cash count by note value (steppers and a numeric keypad, a running total) or as a total, a confirmation, then `record_cash_count` (with `p_replaces_id` when a manager reopened the count). Staff see "Đã ghi nhận" (and that it waits for its shift when not matched yet); owners, managers and staff at the `all` level see the difference for Ca {n} and whether it is over the threshold, from `cash_count_review`. Then the handover (Ca {n} pickers, a note of up to 2,000 characters with the reminder not to write personal details) with `write_handover`, or "Bỏ qua bàn giao", and a result page.
  - **Sự cố và hết hàng** (`src/incidents/`): "Báo sự cố" and "Báo hết hàng" with the category (`incident_categories_of`), the item of a stock-out, the description with the reminder and up to three photos; `report_incident`, then the photos into its prefix and `update_incident`; a report sent without its photos says so and offers to upload them again. The store's open reports and recent handovers are listed for everyone; owners and managers get "Đã xem" and "Đã xử lý" (`acknowledge_incident`, `resolve_incident`, `acknowledge_handover`), never on their own report or handover.
  - **Tiền mặt theo ca** (owners and managers): yesterday's and today's shifts as Ca {n} with the count, the POS amount, the difference and its state, and "Mở lại để đếm lại" (`reopen_cash_count`).
  - Trang chủ's "Việc hôm nay" shows the checklist due and the shift close of the selected store and opens them in Ca làm (`src/shift/home-cards.tsx`, plugged into the home through `HomeTaskSlot` in `src/shift/home-slot.tsx`).
  - Every request goes through `@fnb/zalo-mini`'s operations client with the member's session; refusals are told apart by the database's code, a lost connection says so (and an offline banner shows), and no free text of a note or report is logged.
- Cá nhân (`src/profile/`): the name and role; for a person on the staff roster, "Liên kết số điện thoại" (a purpose screen first, then Zalo's permission prompt; a refusal returns to the tab with nothing changed; success decodes the number with `zalo-phone` and stores it with `link_my_phone`) and "Rút lại đồng ý" (a confirmation popup, then `withdraw_staff_ops_consent`; the stored session is dropped and the app is back at the notice); for everyone, "Quyền riêng tư" and "Điều khoản sử dụng" as popups.
- Privacy and Terms are the tenant's published `privacy_notice` and `mini_app_terms` (in the app's language, else Vietnamese), titled with the document's own title and shown as plain text: links in the text render as words and phone numbers are not tappable (`format-detection` is off). Signed in, they are read through the Data API under RLS; before pairing, from `zalo-session`'s texts request, which the server does not answer yet (the popup then says the text cannot be loaded).
- Signed in, Trang chủ is the role-scoped home (#217, `src/home/`): the greeting and role; the store, one of the brand's stores in the member's scope (a picker when there are several); the freshness stamp of the tenant's latest successful sync ("Cập nhật lúc HH:mm", with the date when it is from an earlier day, "Chưa đồng bộ", or "Chưa rõ lúc cập nhật"); then the cards the role allows:

  | Who | Cards |
  |---|---|
  | owner, manager (and viewer) | today's net revenue ("tr ₫" headline, the exact amount on tap) and invoices, yesterday's reconciliation ("Đã đối soát" or "Chưa đối soát"), the store's opening hours today |
  | staff, `staff_revenue_visibility` = `all` | the same as a manager |
  | staff, `store_totals` | today's net revenue and invoices, opening hours |
  | staff, `none` | opening hours only |

  Today's figures come from `today_store_totals()`, which counts non-voided invoices of each store's business day as Hôm nay does; yesterday's state from `sync_checks`; the stamp from `sync_runs`; all through the member's session under RLS. A day without invoices shows "Chưa có dữ liệu", never 0. The figures reload every 5 minutes and when the app comes back to the foreground.
- The language follows Zalo's (Vietnamese, or English when Zalo is set to English).

The screens, routes and tabs are in `src/app.tsx`, the entry in `src/onboarding/` (the states in `model.ts`, the screens in `screens.tsx`, the gate in `entry-gate.tsx`, the Data API and `zalo-phone` calls in `account.ts`), the popups' texts in `src/legal/`, Cá nhân in `src/profile/`, the home in `src/home/` (cards in `home-screen.tsx`, the role and level rules in `model.ts`, the Data API reads in `source.ts`, the stand-in figures in `sample.ts`), the strings in `src/i18n/vi.json` and `src/i18n/en.json`, the tab icons in `src/icons.tsx`. Everything else (the shell, the session, the popups and result modal, Native Storage, the Data API client, the build) comes from [`@fnb/zalo-mini`](../../packages/zalo-mini/README.md).
- Trang chủ's "Khách thành viên" (#256 to #258) opens the staff loyalty tools for the selected store, each a screen of its own with a back button, the store picker when there are several and the bottom tabs kept at four. "Ghi nhận khách" is the home's one primary action; owners and managers also see the member queue's count and its disputes over 48 hours. Viewers and publishers get no card.
  - **Ghi nhận khách** (`src/check-in/`): "Quét mã thành viên" (Zalo's `scanQRCode` in the Zalo runtime; a refused permission says so and puts the cursor in the field) or one numeric field for a Vietnamese mobile number (10 digits from 0, or the 84 forms) or the 6-digit member code; `staff_record_checkin` with the store and the phone or the code; the result modal for 1.5 s, then "Đang chờ xác nhận" with the expected time, the masked member and links to the two other tools. Each refusal of `staff_record_checkin` has its own sentence: unknown, expired or reused code, another store's code, not a member, a staff phone, the daily cap, a suspended card, the same member recorded at the store within the check-in window (`already_recorded`), a paused or ended programme (`no_programme`), and the earn refusals of a check-in matched at once (`below_minimum`, `excluded_source`, `programme_inactive`, `already_earned`, `invoice_not_completed`; `daily_cap` and `excluded_phone` read as the daily cap and a staff phone); an unknown reason keeps the general sentence. The phone is cleared after the call and never stored.
  - **Kiểm tra ưu đãi** (`src/redemption/`): a typed or scanned reward code to `staff_verify_redemption`; the reward and the rail instruction (the POS discount entry to choose, or the CRM voucher code to type on the total screen), the minimum bill and the hold, pending until the synced invoice confirms it. A used code shows "Mã đã được sử dụng" with the time and store, never the customer (a code another person, or the same person more than the check-in window ago, already checked says when it was checked and that it waits for its receipt: reason `already_verified`); checking it again within the window shows the instruction with "Bạn đã kiểm tra mã này lúc …"; expired, unknown, other-store and returned codes have their own message. The QR payload `fnb-redeem:1:<code>` of the customer app scans as the code.
  - **Tra cứu thành viên** (`src/member-lookup/`): `staff_member_lookup` gives a masked card (stamps, points, tier, pending) and today's visits at the store; "Báo khiếu nại" and "Điều chỉnh" send `staff_request` with a reason; staff adjustments above the threshold wait for a manager.
  - **Hàng chờ thành viên** (`src/approvals/`, owners and managers): unclear invoices (pick one or none, `resolve_loyalty_queue_item`), disputes (accept with an adjustment or refuse) and adjustment requests (approve or refuse), every action with the manager's reason; disputes over 48 hours come first and are flagged. Staff never see it; **Yêu cầu của tôi** shows them their requests with the decision and its reason.
  - The function names, arguments and answers are in `src/loyalty/contract.ts`; `src/loyalty/source.ts` picks the source: the deterministic demo (`src/loyalty/demo.ts`) in stand-in mode (browser mode and CI's Playwright runs); the functions through the Data API in the Zalo runtime, and in the local loop when its stack has every staff loyalty function (`pnpm zalo:dev` checks the database at start and passes `VITE_STAFF_LOYALTY=live`, else `demo` and the screens are marked "Dữ liệu mẫu"). `?loyalty=demo` or `?loyalty=live` in the address switches the local loop either way.
- Phase 15 (#228, #229): announcements, requests to the managers and where notifications arrive. One client (`src/team/client.ts`) calls the functions of `20261118170000_announcements_and_requests.sql` through the Data API in the Zalo runtime and in `pnpm zalo:dev`; stand-in mode answers from the deterministic demo in `src/team/demo.ts`. The screens share `TeamRoute` (`src/team/route.tsx`, the member's stores and formatters) and are listed in `src/team/screens.tsx`; the strings are in `src/team/i18n/`.
  - **Thông báo** (`src/announcements/`, the tab): `announcement_feed`, the notices pinned now first, then the newest; an unread notice has a dot and "Chưa đọc". The detail shows the body as plain text (a web address stays text, never a link) and one primary action, "Đã đọc" (`mark_announcement_read`; the first tap's time is kept); the author gets no "Đã đọc". Owners and managers also see the read rate and who has not read yet (`announcement_read_rate`, `announcement_readers`, which give a store-limited manager their own stores' staff only) and "Soạn thông báo": title (120 characters), plain-text body (4,000), the stores (theirs, all ticked at first) and a pin of 1, 3, 7 or 30 days, then `publish_announcement` and a result modal for 1.5 s.
  - Trang chủ shows the pinned notices of the selected store at the top, with the unread count, each opening its detail under Thông báo (`src/team/home-cards.tsx`, plugged in through `HomeTeamSlot` in `src/team/home-slot.tsx`).
  - **Yêu cầu** (`src/requests/`): Trang chủ's "Yêu cầu" card opens **Gửi yêu cầu** (the store, a kind from `approval_request_kinds_of`, details of up to 1,000 characters, `submit_request`, a result modal for 1.5 s) and **Yêu cầu của tôi** (`my_requests`: each request's state, the decision time and the reason of a refusal). For owners and managers the card also shows the pending count of every store they manage (`approval_queue`) and opens **Duyệt yêu cầu**: the oldest first, the member's own requests left out; "Duyệt", or "Từ chối" with a reason of up to 500 characters, through `decide_request`, a result modal that closes after 1.5 s, and "Yêu cầu này đã được quản lý khác xử lý" when someone decided first.
  - **Nhận thông báo** (`src/notifications/`, from Cá nhân), through the member functions of `docs/api.md` section 3.5: the member's `notifications` consent (since when, or when they stopped) and their own destinations (receiving, waiting for the code or for "ĐỒNG Ý", failing or stopped with its reason, and how many kinds of message are muted), read with `my_notifications`. "Liên kết Zalo Bot" first shows the phase 12 notice `ops-notifications-v1` word for word; only "Tạo mã liên kết" calls `start_bot_link` with that version, and the code then shows with a countdown of its 15 minutes and the three steps (open Zalo and find the Bot by name, send the code, answer "ĐỒNG Ý"), never a link; an expired code offers a new one after the notice again; consent is recorded only by the member's own "ĐỒNG Ý" in the Bot chat. "Tắt từng loại tin" lists, per destination that is not stopped, the rules the member's role receives as ticked boxes; unticking one mutes it (`set_my_muted_rules`) and a refusal puts the box back. "Dừng nhận thông báo" asks for a confirmation, then `stop_my_notifications` stops every message at once and says how many waiting messages were cancelled; the staff app session and the `staff_ops` consent stay. "Theo dõi OA" after its one-line purpose, through the SDK's `followOA` with the brand's OA from `my_follow_oa`, where a refusal changes nothing else ("Chưa sẵn sàng" only when the brand has no OA); how to join the store's Zalo group.

Where the home reads its data: in the Zalo runtime from `VITE_SUPABASE_URL`; with `pnpm zalo:dev` (every mode) from the dev server's `/supabase` path, which Vite proxies to the local API; in stand-in browser mode from fixed sample figures.

## Commands

```sh
pnpm --filter @fnb/zalo-staff dev        # browser mode on http://127.0.0.1:5741 with the mocked SDK and a stand-in session
pnpm --filter @fnb/zalo-staff build      # vite build (Testing stage), then the size budgets
pnpm --filter @fnb/zalo-staff test       # Vitest with jsdom
pnpm --filter @fnb/zalo-staff e2e:install
pnpm --filter @fnb/zalo-staff test:e2e   # Playwright on 5742 (plus E2E_PORT_OFFSET)
```

In browser mode the address chooses the stand-in session: `?session=unpaired` is a first open (onboarding, the notice, pairing), `?session=notice` a paired person asked for the notice again, `?session=failed` a lost connection; `&delay=<ms>` holds every session answer; the code `000000` is refused, `999999` answers the 15-minute wait and any other code pairs; `&notice=changed` publishes a newer notice while the person pairs; `?consent=outdated|missing|not_required` sets a stored session's notice state (owners default to `not_required`, off the roster); `?phone=refused` refuses Zalo's phone prompt; `?lang=en` sets the mocked Zalo language. The stand-in member is a synthetic manager of two sample stores; `?role=owner` or `?role=staff` (one store) changes the role, `&stores=one` keeps an owner or manager to the first store, `?revenue=all|store_totals|none` sets the staff revenue level of the sample tenant (default `all`), and `&dataDelay=<ms>` holds the home's loading state, the Cá nhân calls and every Ca làm call. Ca làm's sample data (three checklists, two reports, a handover and two days of cash per shift) answers like the database, refusals included; `?camera=refused` refuses the mocked camera, which otherwise opens a file input, `?upload=fail-once` loses the first photo upload, `?checklist=taken` puts the opening checklist in someone else's hands, and `?count=unmatched` leaves a cash count waiting for its shift. The loyalty demo answers on any store: the member code `246810` records the first sample member once (`135790` is expired, `975310` belongs to another store), the phones `0900001234` and `0900005678` are members, `0900003333` has reached the daily cap, `0900004444` is suspended and `0900000000` is a staff phone; the reward codes `UD2468` (POS discount) and `UD3579` (CRM voucher) are valid once, `UD1111` was used at another store, `UD0000` has expired, `UD9753` belongs to another store and `UD5555` was returned; the queue holds an unclear invoice, a dispute 50 hours old and an adjustment above the threshold (2 stamps, 20 points). In browser mode the scan is a stand-in that reads the demo member or reward code; `?scan=refused` refuses the scan permission, `?scan=cancel` cancels it and `&scanCode=<text>` scans that text. The phase 15 demo has four sample notices (one pinned for three days whose body holds a web address as text, one unread, one read, and for owners and managers one of their own), five synthetic colleagues for the read rate, two of the member's requests (one approved, one refused with its reason), three requests waiting for owners and managers, one Zalo Bot destination with an active `notifications` consent, the role's rules to mute, the Bot `Bot Mẫu` and the OA `mock-oa-sample`; `?notify=unlinked` starts the member with no destination and no consent, so "Liên kết Zalo Bot" leads through the notice to the sample codes (`FNB-MAUTHU27` first, at most five an hour); `?follow=refused` refuses the mocked follow.

A build for the Zalo runtime needs `VITE_ZALO_RUNTIME=zalo`, `VITE_BRAND_KEY`, `VITE_SUPABASE_URL`, `VITE_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` and the brand's generated `app-config.json` in this folder (gitignored); `deploy-mini-apps.yml` prepares them ([operations.md, 4.17](../../docs/operations.md#417-mini-app-deploys)). The other variables are in [development.md](../../docs/development.md), section 3.
