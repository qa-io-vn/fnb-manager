# Customer Mini App (`@fnb/zalo-customer`)

Purpose and audience: the customer Zalo Mini App of track C, one build per brand from configuration and listed in Zalo's search (`mini_apps.browsable` true). For developers of the customer screens, for the backend work behind the loyalty functions it calls, and for reviewers of Zalo's design rules. Phase 13 (#208) built the scaffold; phase 18a (#261 to #265) adds the loyalty app: browse first, join with the phone number after a purpose screen, a wallet with stamps and points, a dynamic member code and receipt claims; phase 18b (#272) adds the voucher wallet: rewards redeemed with stamps or points, a single-use code with its countdown, "Đã dùng" after the till and "Đã hoàn lại" for an unused hold; #266 and #267 add the builds per brand with their performance gates, the listing check and the per-store QR standees, with "Xóa dữ liệu của tôi" and a pass over the empty, loading, error and offline states. It runs on its demo data, and with `pnpm zalo:dev --app customer --live` on the customer functions of the local stack (proven end to end in `e2e/loyalty-local.spec.ts`). Nothing has run inside Zalo yet, because the owner's Zalo accounts do not exist yet (#8).

Last updated 2026-11-28 (the customer accepts a time the store proposed, in the ordering build; before: company invoices, C18, in the ordering build; ordering at the table behind VITE_ORDERING for tester builds; live functions, closed joining, voucher wallet, the server's answers for closed joining and returned holds, a synthetic staff login; "Xóa dữ liệu của tôi", the offline notice and the no-store state, store links in any case, QR standees, the listing check and the standee paint gate)

## What it shows

Three bottom tabs: **Trang chủ**, **Thẻ thành viên** and **Tài khoản** ("Home", "Member card", "Account"). Nothing asks for a permission at launch: the app signs in with the Zalo user id alone (a visitor session) and everything below works without a phone number.

- **Trang chủ** (`src/home/`): the programme (name, summary, reward, stamp and point rules, welcome stamps), "Tham gia thành viên" as the one primary action (or "Xem thẻ thành viên" for a member) and "Nhận điểm từ hóa đơn"; the stores with today's opening hours; the menu highlights, each marked "Xem tại quầy" and never with a price; "Chính sách bảo mật" and "Điều khoản sử dụng".
- **When joining is closed** (`src/home/join-actions.tsx`, `joinBlock` in `src/home/model.ts`): without an open programme (none at all, `paused`, `ended`, or a period that does not cover today) or without a published privacy notice (`legal.policy_version` null), browsing stays usable and "Tham gia thành viên" and "Nhận điểm từ hóa đơn" are disabled everywhere (home, store page, card tab, account tab) with one sentence saying why, in Vietnamese and English; with no programme at all the home shows "Chương trình thành viên" and "Quán chưa mở chương trình thành viên." The join screen opened anyway says "Chưa tham gia được" with the reason and never asks for the phone. A join refused with `no_programme`, or one that fails while the programme closed, reloads the home and words it.
- **Store page** (`/cua-hang/:code`): address, the week's opening hours with today in bold, the menu served there, and the member code or joining. A store link `?store=<code>&src=<qr|standee|receipt>` opens it: read with `getRouteParams` on load and from the `OpenApp` event when Zalo brings the app back with a new link (`src/data/store-link.tsx`, the pure parsing and building in `src/data/link.ts`). The code is kept as written and matched against the home's stores exactly, then ignoring case when only one store matches (`storeByCode` in `src/home/model.ts`), so `?store=MAU-1` opens `mau-1` and a POS code in capitals still works; the store's own code is what the member code, the claim, the rewards and joining send. The linked or last opened store is the one the member code and the claim form start with; joining sends it only when it is one of the home's stores, so a link to a closed store never refuses the join with `unknown_store`.
- **No store yet**: when `customer_home` lists no store, the home, Mã thành viên, Nhận điểm từ hóa đơn and Đổi quà say "Chưa có cửa hàng nào nhận thành viên. Hãy quay lại sau." instead of an empty picker.
- **Offline**: while the phone is offline (`navigator.onLine` and the `online` and `offline` events), every screen starts with "Bạn đang ngoại tuyến. Thông tin trên màn hình có thể chưa mới; ứng dụng tự tải lại khi có mạng." (`src/data/offline.tsx`); when the connection is back, a failed session starts again, a failed home loads again, and every screen that failed to load reloads itself. A request that fails meanwhile says "Mất kết nối mạng. Kiểm tra mạng rồi thử lại." with "Thử lại".
- **Privacy and Terms** (`src/legal/`): in-app popups titled "Chính sách bảo mật Mini App <Tên>" and "Điều khoản sử dụng Mini App <Tên>" (the name is the Mini App title from `app-config.json`), naming the legal entity and the version and date of the tenant's published text. Links in the text render as words.
- **Joining** (`src/join/`), from "Tham gia thành viên" or, for a visitor, "Nhận điểm từ hóa đơn" (the only two actions that lead to the phone number):
  1. a purpose screen: what is collected (the Zalo phone number, display name and user id, and purchases), why, how long (the programme's retention), who processes it, a link to the Privacy popup, "Tiếp tục" or "Để sau";
  2. a consent screen: the loyalty consent (required, unticked), a separate marketing switch (never pre-ticked), the policy version; "Tham gia" stays disabled until the loyalty consent is ticked;
  3. "Tham gia" asks Zalo for the phone number (`getPhoneNumber`) and sends its token with both consents, the policy version, the screen version (`join-1`), the store and the time the consent screen was shown to `zalo-session` in one request;
  4. a welcome page with the welcome stamps and the follow-OA step ("Quan tâm", `followOA` with the brand's Official Account), then the card, or the claim form when the person came to claim.

  Declining the purpose, cancelling the consent or refusing Zalo's phone prompt returns to Trang chủ with a short explanation; nothing is sent or stored, and browsing stays usable. An expired phone token asks again; a changed policy reloads the text.
- **Thẻ thành viên** (`src/wallet/`): the member card the law asks for (programme name, display name, masked phone, member since, number of purchases), a stamps ring per card (confirmed, then pending stamps dashed), points with the pending and the next expiry, the tier and the points to the next one, the receipts sent with their state, and the history with "Đang chờ xác nhận", "Đã xác nhận" or "Đã hoàn lại" and how each earn was matched (phone number, member code, receipt, voucher, welcome). A visitor sees the programme and "Tham gia thành viên".
- **Mã thành viên** (`/ma-thanh-vien`): a store picker (the linked store preselected), the QR code (`uqr`) and the 6-digit code, "Mã đổi sau {n} giây" and "Dùng được đến HH:mm:ss". The code is fetched again at its refresh time (60 seconds) and lasts 2 minutes; when it cannot be refreshed the old one stays until it expires, then "Mã đã hết hạn" with "Lấy mã mới".
- **Nhận điểm từ hóa đơn** (`/nhan-diem`, `src/claim/`): store, receipt number (hint "Số hóa đơn in trên bill (thường 6 chữ số)."; iPOS prints the day of the month and a 4-digit daily sequence on a store that restarts its numbers each day, [ipos-fabi.md](../../docs/connectors/ipos-fabi.md#loyalty-fields)); at a store whose numbers restart each day (`receipt_numbers_reset` `daily` on the home), once the field is left or a send is tried, a gentle notice says the number should start with the chosen date's day of month ("Số hóa đơn ở cửa hàng này bắt đầu bằng ngày trong tháng (19 cho ngày đã chọn). Hãy xem lại số hóa đơn hoặc ngày trên hóa đơn. Bạn vẫn gửi được."); a shorter number of digits is read with its leading zeros, other characters are not checked, and sending is never blocked (`receiptDayMismatch` in `src/claim/model.ts`), the total (numeric keypad, grouped digits) and the date (today, yesterday or the day before), the claims left today; problems show after the first try; the result is pending ("Đang chờ xác nhận"), matched or refused with its reason; no more than 3 a day.
- **Đổi quà** (`/doi-qua`, `src/wallet/vouchers.tsx`, reached from Thẻ thành viên): the store picker (the linked store preselected) and the rewards valid there, each described by its kind and items ("Tặng …", "Giảm …%", "Nâng cỡ miễn phí", "Giảm trực tiếp trên hóa đơn") and never by an amount, with its cost in stamps or points, the stores it is limited to, and either "Bạn đủ dấu và điểm để đổi quà này." or why not ("Cần thêm 2 dấu để đổi.", already held, limit reached, the store's rail not ready, a suspended card). "Đổi quà" opens a confirmation with the cost, the store and the hold ("Mã được giữ cho bạn trong 24 giờ …"); "Xác nhận đổi" calls `customer_redeem` and opens the code; a refusal is worded per reason. A held reward offers "Xem mã" instead.
- **Ví voucher** (`/vi-voucher`, `/voucher/:id`): the redemptions, held first: "Đang giữ" with the time left, "Hết thời gian giữ" (the stamps go back tonight), "Đã dùng" with the time, "Đã hoàn lại" with what went back, "Đã hủy". A held code shows its QR (`fnb-redeem:1:<code>`), the code, "Còn HH:MM:SS", "Dùng được đến …", the instruction to show it to the cashier and, once staff checked it, a note; it reloads when the app returns to the foreground, every 30 seconds while held and when the hold ends.
- **Tài khoản** (`src/settings/`): the member's name and masked phone; "Dừng nhận tin" with a confirmation (stops promotions at once, stamps and points unchanged); "Xóa dữ liệu của tôi" (below); Privacy and Terms. While the session starts it shows "Đang tải…", and when it cannot start, the error with "Thử lại". In a Development build only, the phone-consent probe of phase 13 sits at the bottom (`src/probe/`, marker `phone-probe`, refused in a Testing build).
- **Xóa dữ liệu của tôi** (`src/settings/settings-screen.tsx`): the card says what is deleted (the member card, phone number, stamps, points, rewards, receipts sent and the consents on record). "Xóa dữ liệu của tôi" opens "Xóa dữ liệu của bạn?": the request goes to the shop owner; once carried out the card is closed and the data deleted for good, unused stamps, points and rewards lost; "Chủ quán thực hiện yêu cầu trong thời hạn pháp luật quy định: phản hồi trong 2 ngày làm việc và xóa xong trong 20 ngày (30 ngày nếu có bên xử lý dữ liệu tham gia)."; until then the person stays a member. "Gửi yêu cầu xóa" stays disabled until "Tôi hiểu dấu, điểm và ưu đãi chưa dùng sẽ mất." is ticked; "Giữ nguyên" closes it and sends nothing. The request calls `customer_request_deletion()`; the card then shows its state ("Chủ quán đã nhận yêu cầu", "Chủ quán đang xử lý", "Đã xóa xong" or "Yêu cầu không được thực hiện"), "Gửi lúc HH:mm dd/mm/yyyy · Mã yêu cầu XXXXXXXX" (the first 8 characters of the request id) and the deadlines. The card reads the request from `customer_wallet()` (`deletion_request`), so it is there after a restart: the open request, else the latest one, and a refused one ("Yêu cầu không được thực hiện") offers a new request. The answer sent in this session is shown at once and kept across the tabs (`CustomerDataProvider`) until the wallet reloads. With the server's dates the deadlines read "Chủ quán phản hồi chậm nhất ngày dd/mm/yyyy." (while the request is received) and "Dữ liệu được xóa xong chậm nhất ngày dd/mm/yyyy."; an answer without them keeps the durations above. An owner carries it out with `dsr_delete` ([operations.md](../../docs/operations.md), deletion of a customer).
- The session lives in Native Storage under `fnb.customer-session` (`@fnb/zalo-mini`); no LocalStorage, SessionStorage, IndexedDB or cookie. The language follows Zalo's.

## The loyalty contract

The wire shapes live in one module, `@fnb/zalo-mini/loyalty` (`packages/zalo-mini/src/loyalty/contract.ts`): the function names (`CUSTOMER_FUNCTIONS`), the argument builders, a reader per answer that refuses anything malformed, and the constants (`MEMBER_CODE_REFRESH_SECONDS` 60, `MEMBER_CODE_VALID_SECONDS` 120, `MAX_CLAIMS_PER_DAY` 3, `CLAIM_WINDOW_DAYS` 3, the code, store, receipt and version patterns). `createRestLoyaltyClient(rest)` calls them through the Data API with the customer's session. Snake case is the wire; the readers return camel case.

### The customer session: `zalo-session` with `kind: "customer"`

`POST /functions/v1/zalo-session` with the publishable key:

```json
{ "kind": "customer", "access_token": "<Zalo access token>", "brand": "brand-1" }
```

and, to join, the same with:

```json
"join": {
  "phone_token": "<getPhoneNumber token>",
  "loyalty_consent": true,
  "marketing_consent": false,
  "policy_version": "privacy-1",
  "screen_version": "join-1",
  "store": "mau-1",
  "shown_at": "2026-11-19T03:00:00.000Z"
}
```

The answer (200):

```json
{
  "status": "customer",
  "access_token": "…", "refresh_token": "…", "expires_at": 1795000000,
  "customer": { "tenant_id": "<uuid>", "customer_id": "<uuid or null>", "display_name": "<Zalo name or null>" },
  "joined": { "membership_id": "<uuid>", "welcome_stamps": 1, "already_member": false }
}
```

`joined` comes only with a join. Refusals are `{error, request_id}`: 400 `invalid_kind`, `invalid_join`, `invalid_phone_token`, `loyalty_consent_required`; 401 `zalo_token_refused`; 404 `unknown_store`; 409 `policy_outdated`, `phone_token_used`, `phone_in_use`, `no_programme` (no open programme to join; nothing is written); 422 `phone_token_refused` (an expired or unknown token); 502 `zalo_unavailable`; 503 `backend_unavailable`.

What the function does (`supabase/functions/zalo-session/customer.ts`): checks the Zalo token with the graph's `me`; calls `zalo_customer_sign_in`; on `login_needed` creates a login `customer-<uuid>@zalo-customer.invalid` through the Auth admin API with `app_metadata` `{kind: "customer", tenant_id, customer_id: null}` and calls again with its id; for a join, exchanges the phone token once with the graph (`me/info`, the same exchange as `zalo-phone`), normalises it to E.164 and calls `customer_join`; then sets `app_metadata` to `{kind: "customer", tenant_id, customer_id}` (so RLS can read `auth.jwt() -> 'app_metadata'`) and mints the session. The phone, tokens, ids and names are redacted from its logs.

## Ordering at the table (tester builds only, `VITE_ORDERING=on`)

Phase 1 of the owner's plan on the native till ([native-till.md](../../docs/deep-dives/native-till.md), ADR-060 to ADR-065). Zalo's review asks for the Checkout SDK as soon as an app shows prices and takes orders (ADR-058, ADR-062), so ordering is a build flag: `VITE_ORDERING=on` for the Development and Testing builds testers open, off by default. A build without it is the loyalty app above, unchanged, and the build fails when it still holds an ordering screen (`orderingOnlyMarkers` in `vite.config.ts`); `main.tsx` loads the ordering shell (`src/ordering/ordering-app.tsx`) only with the flag. Nothing in the ordering screens prices an order: they show the menu's prices through `PriceText`, and the server prices again at placement.

- **Tabs:** Trang chủ · Thực đơn · Tích điểm · Tôi (four, Zalo's limit). A screen pushed over the tabs (an order, the history, the member code) shows no tab active, so every tab opens its screen from it.
- **Table QR** `?store=<link key>&src=table&t=<token>` (at launch and on `OpenApp`) opens the menu; the token goes to `customer_table` and the screens show the table the server names ("Gọi món cho Bàn 12 · Tầng 1"), never a label from the address. A forged, rotated or unknown token shows "Mã bàn này không còn dùng được" with a scan button. The table stays for the session (a new QR replaces it), so "Gọi thêm" orders again at the same table.
- **C01** greeting with the table, two tiles (order at the table or scan its code; the menu), the stamp card's progress, the legal texts. **C02** search with or without Vietnamese marks, a sticky row of sections, the lowest price of each item, "+" (the default choice, or C03 when a choice is needed), the basket bar; `customer_menu_version` every 3 seconds while the menu, an item, the basket or the confirmation is open, so an item switched sold out leaves the menu within 5 seconds; C15 above the menu when the shop is closed, a notice for a pause and the other holds, C16 offline with nothing to show. **C03** sizes with their prices, option groups within their limits, a note of at most 100 characters (as Postgres counts them; no control character or bidirectional override), the quantity 1 to 20. **C04** the lines (at most 30), a line whose item just sold out flagged with "Bỏ món" and the order blocked until it goes (acceptance criterion 2), a price that changed, offline. **C05** the server's total from `customer_check_cart`; "Trả tại quầy" always works; "Ví ZaloPay" and "Chuyển khoản QR" can be chosen only in a build with `VITE_ONLINE_PAYMENT=on` and where the store offers them (its ordering state's `payment_methods`), otherwise they are shown disabled with a note; "Đặt món" sends `customer_place_order` with one `request_id` per basket, total and way to pay, kept until an answer comes back, so a double tap or a retry after a lost answer gives one order (criterion 3); ## Demo data and the data source

`src/data/source.ts` picks the source the way the staff app does: with the stand-in data endpoint (plain browser mode and `pnpm zalo:dev --app customer`), a deterministic demo world (`src/demo/world.ts`, fixtures in `src/demo/fixtures.ts`) that answers the session request and every function above at the wire level, so the same readers and REST client run as in production; with a Supabase endpoint (`--live`, or the Zalo runtime), the Data API through `createRestLoyaltyClient`. The demo world has two synthetic stores (`mau-1`, `mau-2`), four rewards (a free coffee for 3 stamps, a cookie for 50 points once per member, 10 % off for 150 points at `mau-1`, a size upgrade for 5 stamps) held for 24 hours with the codes of the fallback rail, four menu highlights, a placeholder privacy and terms text, a programme of 10 stamps, 1 welcome stamp, a 72-hour claim window and 3 claims a day, and two synced receipts that a claim matches at once (`1001` at `mau-1` for 85000 today; yesterday's day of month then `0042` at `mau-2` for 42000 yesterday); `mau-1` numbers its receipts by year and `mau-2` by day; any other receipt stays pending. Claims follow `loyalty_submit_claim`: leading zeros ignored, every submission counting toward the day's 3, and the refusals `outside_window`, `daily_cap`, `already_claimed` and `amount_mismatch`.

Address switches in browser mode: `?member=1` starts as a member with a history (3 of 10 stamps, one pending, a reversed earn, 105 points); `&marketing=1` with the promotion consent; `&vouchers=1` adds a used and a returned voucher (55 points left); `?privacy=none` has no published privacy notice and `?programme=none|paused|ended` no open programme (joining disabled); `?claims=<n>` with n claims already sent today; `?phone=refused` refuses Zalo's phone prompt; `?follow=refused` refuses following the OA; `?session=failed` loses the connection; `&dataDelay=<ms>` holds every answer; `?stores=none` answers a home without stores; `?store=<code>&src=qr` is a store link; `?lang=en` sets the mocked Zalo language; `&deletion=received|in_progress|rejected` (with `?member=1`) starts with a deletion request sent the day before, as after a restart.

### Store links and QR standees (#267)

Each store's link opens its page: `https://zalo.me/s/<customer Mini App id>/?store=<link key>&src=<qr|standee|receipt>`, the link key being the store's code from `customer_home` (`stores.code`, else the POS `external_id`), the same key `customer_member_code` and `customer_submit_claim` take. `storeDeepLink` and `miniAppLink` (`src/data/link.ts`) build it and `storeLinkFrom` reads it back, so the two cannot drift.

`scripts/standees.ts` writes one A6 standee per store (105 × 148 mm, SVG, and PDF with `--pdf` through Playwright's Chromium): the brand's band in its light header colour with its name, the store's name, the QR of the store's link with `src=standee` (`uqr`, error correction Q and a 4-module quiet zone, drawn by the app's own `qrPath`), "Quét mã bằng Zalo" and "Tham gia thành viên, tích dấu và điểm mỗi lần mua tại quán", and the link in small print. No price, no phone number.

```sh
pnpm --filter @fnb/zalo-customer standees --brand brand-1                      # synthetic config, stores mau-1 and mau-2, SVG
pnpm --filter @fnb/zalo-customer standees --brand brand-1 --pdf --out D:/standees/brand-1
pnpm --filter @fnb/zalo-customer standees --brand brand-1 --store mau-2 --link-base "<the address pnpm zalo:dev --app customer --lan prints>"
pnpm --filter @fnb/zalo-customer standees --brand brand-1 --config D:/private/brand-1.json --link-base "https://zalo.me/s/<id>/?env=DEVELOPMENT&version=<v>"
```

- **The brand and its stores** come from `packages/zalo-mini/config` (the synthetic brands; the Mini App row's `header_title_vi`, `header_color_light`, `text_color_light`, and from `synthetic-listings.json` its `id` and `stores: [{code, name}]`); `--config` (repeatable) reads another file, such as the owner's brand file kept outside the repository with an `id` and `stores` on its customer row. `--store <code>[=<name>]` (repeatable) names the stores instead, the name taken from the configuration when it lists that code.
- **The link.** Without `--link-base` the QR opens the customer Mini App's published link, which needs its Mini App id; `--link-base` replaces `https://zalo.me/s/<id>/` and keeps its own parameters, for a Development or Testing version's link from `zmp deploy`, or for the local loop: with the address `pnpm zalo:dev --app customer --lan` prints, its one-time key included, the phone's camera opens that store's page in Safari (a store of the demo world, `mau-1` or `mau-2`; with `--live`, `sim-1` or `sim-2`).
- **Output** goes to `<temp>/fnb-standees/<brand>` or `--out`, which must be outside the repository (the command refuses a folder inside it).
- **Proof.** `scripts/standee.test.ts` checks the link format, its round trip through `storeLinkFrom`, that the QR drawn is exactly the link's QR, the escaping and the folder rule; `e2e/browse.spec.ts` opens a standee's link made for the test build and lands on that store's page. The scan of a printed standee inside Zalo waits for a Development version (#8, #268).

### Builds per brand, budgets and performance gates (#266)

- **From configuration only.** CI's "Mini App builds" job (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) writes each synthetic brand's `app-config.json` from `packages/zalo-mini/config/synthetic-brands.json`, builds the Testing stage per brand into `dist/brand-1` and `dist/brand-2`, fails when the two `app-config.json` files differ outside the configured fields (`app-config --compare`), and runs `check-budget.mjs` on both. On 2026-11-19 each customer build is 4 files, 0.58 MB of 10 MB, its largest file 0.44 MB of 3 MB, with no ECharts in the dependency graph; brand-2's build carries "Thành viên Quán Mẫu Hai" and neither carries the Development probe.
- **Native Storage only.** Biome's `noRestrictedGlobals` refuses `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` and `indexedDB` (`biome.json`), `packages/zalo-mini/test/unit/no-web-storage.test.ts` scans the shipped sources for them and `document.cookie`, and every Playwright screen fails on a LocalStorage, SessionStorage, cookie or IndexedDB access.
- **Performance gates.** `e2e/budget.spec.ts` (project `budget`) loads two first screens on a throttled mid-range phone (4× CPU slowdown, about 1.6 Mbit/s down, 150 ms latency, no cache) and fails at a largest contentful paint of 2.5 s or a load of 1.5 s or more: the home, and a standee's store page (`?store=mau-1&src=standee`), the first screen of every scan. Measured on this PC on 2026-11-19: home LCP about 2.1 s and load about 1.3 s, the store page about 2.0 s and 1.2 s. The margin is small: on a PC busy with other builds the home measured 3.2 s and 1.6 s once, and passed again alone. The same numbers are measured by hand in the Zalo simulator for each release candidate and recorded in the release notes.
- **Testing versions.** `deploy-mini-apps.yml` deploys a Testing version of every configured Mini App on a `mini-v<version>` tag with `zmp-cli` and the developer token from `zalo_tokens` ([operations.md, 4.17](../../docs/operations.md#417-mini-app-deploys)). It has never run: it waits for the owner's Zalo accounts and Mini App ids (#8). Before the first tag: the brand's rows and Mini App ids are set; `pnpm --filter @fnb/zalo-mini listing --config <brand file>` passes and the owner ticks its checklist; the project's `https://<project ref>.supabase.co` is an allowed domain of the customer Mini App too; both apps' `build` and `test:e2e` pass on the commit; a Development version opens on the owner's phone and a printed standee opens its store (#268).

### The full local trial on the simulated till (`loyalty-sim`)

The synthetic tenant `loyalty-sim` (stores `sim-1` and `sim-2`, an active synthetic programme, published privacy and terms texts) is the one the simulated till accepts; `live-local` has no approved programme and is never used here.

1. `pnpm loyalty:sale -- --setup --tenant loyalty-sim` once, if the tenant is missing.
2. `pnpm zalo:dev --app customer --live` (add `--tunnel` for the iPhone). `--live` targets `loyalty-sim` by default and, on the first run, seeds a synthetic `brand-1` into it: the brand over its stores, a mock Zalo App (`mock-sim-app-1`), OA and the staff and customer Mini Apps (`packages/zalo/src/local-dev/seed.ts`). 3. In the app: "Tham gia thành viên", tick the loyalty consent, "Tham gia" (the mock phone token joins as 0900000789), and see the welcome stamps on Thẻ thành viên.
4. Ring a sale: `pnpm loyalty:sale -- --tenant loyalty-sim --amount 60000`. It prints the store, business date, receipt number and amount.
5. In the app: "Nhận điểm từ hóa đơn", pick that store, type the receipt number and 60000, keep the date, "Gửi hóa đơn". A synced receipt matches at once; otherwise the claim shows "Đang chờ xác nhận" until the reconciliation.

### A staff login for `loyalty-sim`: `pnpm zalo:dev setup-staff`

`loyalty-sim` has no member logins of its own, so the staff app has nobody to open as. `pnpm zalo:dev setup-staff` (`packages/zalo/src/local-dev/staff-setup.ts`, and `seedSyntheticManager` in `seed.ts`) makes one on the local stack and is safe to run again:

- it refuses every tenant but a synthetic one (`loyalty-sim`, the default, or `zalo-it-…`; never `live-local`), a tenant registered as fed by the real iPOS API, and a `SUPABASE_URL` or `DATABASE_URL` that is not on 127.0.0.1;
- it seeds the synthetic `brand-1` first when the tenant has none (as `--app customer --live` does), because the staff loop needs it;
- it creates the Auth login `loyalty-sim-manager@example.com` with a generated password and makes it a `manager` of every active store (`sim-1` and `sim-2`); a rerun keeps the login and its password and only puts the membership back to manager of every active store;
- it adds no `staff_roster` row: a member who is not on the roster signs in after the loop's one-time binding code without the staff notice, as the managers of the local Playwright spec do.

It prints the password only when it creates the login, or with `--new-password`, which sets a new one:

```text
zalo:dev setup-staff: loyalty-sim on the local stack
  brand-1: already seeded
  manager login: loyalty-sim-manager@example.com, manager of Synthetic store 1 and Synthetic store 2 (created)
  password: <24 random characters>
    shown once: it is kept nowhere but as a hash in the local stack's auth.users; it also signs in to the web app on the local stack
  no staff roster row: a member who is not on the roster signs in after the one-time binding code, without the staff notice
open the staff app as this manager:
  pnpm zalo:dev --tenant loyalty-sim --member loyalty-sim-manager@example.com --mock-user mock-sim-manager-1
```

The password is written to no file. The staff loop needs only the e-mail; the password is for the web app, and `setup-staff --new-password` replaces a lost one. `--tenant` and `--brand` pick another synthetic tenant or brand.

To remove a trial customer, use the data-subject deletion (`dsr_delete`, or `app.dsr_erase_customer` as the database owner), as `e2e/loyalty-fixture.ts` does; consent records refuse any other delete.

A build for the Zalo runtime needs `VITE_ZALO_RUNTIME=zalo`, `VITE_BRAND_KEY`, `VITE_SUPABASE_URL`, `VITE_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` and the brand's generated `app-config.json` in this folder (gitignored), as for the staff app ([apps/zalo-staff/README.md](../zalo-staff/README.md)).

## What the server still owes

The functions above are live (migrations `20261119200000` to `20261119220000`, `20261119310000` and `20261119600000`) and the app reads their answers field by field. The three open points of 2026-11-19 are closed by migration `20261119600000_customer_app_answers` and `zalo-session` (pgTAP `93_customer_app_answers.sql`, Deno `customer.test.ts`):

- `customer_join` answers `{status: refused, reason: no_programme}` when no active programme covers the tenant's today, and `zalo-session` maps it to 409 `no_programme`, which the app words.
- `customer_home` returns `programme.status` (active, paused, ended), or `programme: null` without a programme, so a paused or ended programme no longer looks open.
- `customer_wallet` reads the `redeem` row of a released hold as `reversed` (and of a used voucher as `confirmed`) and gives the `redemption_released` adjustment no path; the demo world answers the same.

The open points of the customer app (2026-11-19, #266, #267 and the deletion flow) are closed by migrations `20261119970000_customer_deletion_request_state` and `20261119970100_store_receipt_numbers_reset` (pgTAP `115_customer_deletion_state.sql`, `116_store_receipt_numbers.sql`):

- **The deletion request after a restart.** `customer_wallet()` answers `deletion_request`: the open request, else the latest (a refused one too), else null, so the card shows it after a restart and offers a new request only when none is open.
- **The deadlines as dates.** `customer_request_deletion()` and the wallet's `deletion_request` carry `first_response_due_at` and `completion_due_at` as the ledger's trigger filled them, and the card shows the dates.
- **Receipt numbering per store.** `customer_home` gives each store `receipt_numbers_reset` (the owner's `stores.tran_no_reset_period` when set, else `stores.receipt_numbers_reset`, which the iPOS sync now fills from the stores master's `extra_data.reset_tran_no_period`), and the claim form warns at a `daily` store, never blocking. The server's matching (`loyalty_match_claim`) is unchanged: the full number typed already carries the day, so the prefix adds nothing to the match, and moving a claim to another date would change what the customer claimed.

What the ordering screens took from the server on 2026-11-28 (native-till.md section 16):

- **When an order should be ready.** `ready_estimate_at` on `customer_order` and `customer_orders`: C06 says "Dự kiến xong lúc HH:MM, khoảng N phút nữa" while the order is accepted or being made, "Sắp xong" once the time has passed, and the late line five minutes later; C14 shows the time on each open order.
- **The member code of the Zalo account (C20).** `customer_stamp_code` for any login, member or not: the QR (drawn as an SVG path, its payload in no attribute, address or log) and the 8 digits, "Mã làm mới sau mm:ss", a new code at the refresh time while online and with "Làm mới mã"; offline the code on the screen keeps working until its expiry and says so, then "Mã đã hết hạn"; kept in memory beside the session only (never in storage); the stamp card under it and the counter's attach announced (`customer_stamp_card`'s `attached`, read again every 10 seconds while the screen is open).
- **Deleting a login that never joined (C19).** `customer_delete_account(true)` at once behind the confirmation, refused while an order is open (the screen says how many) or a refund is owed; a member still sends the request (`customer_request_deletion`). The done screen gives the time, when the backups are cleared and that invoices stay, then starts a new visitor session.
- **A copy of the customer's data (C19).** `customer_export_data` shown section by section in the app and copied as one JSON text. A Mini App cannot freely write a file: zmp-sdk's `downloadFile` needs a permission granted to the app and an https file, and the share sheet sends to a chat (an outbound action the review rules hold back), so neither is used.
- **The customer's requests.** Tôi → "Đặt chỗ của bạn" lists `customer_requests` with the time the store proposed and its message, and cancels a new, proposed or confirmed one (`customer_cancel_request`). Making a booking in the app is not built yet.
